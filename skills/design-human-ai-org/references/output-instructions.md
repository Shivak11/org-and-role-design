# Deliver the participant's readbacks

Use [recording-guide.md](recording-guide.md) for the normal file-capable path. Keep answers in one editable session record. The helper checks the selected stage, generates repeated presentation fields and writes a stage snapshot. Its final check is required before requesting confirmation; an older renderer's valid-record message does not establish completeness.

## What the participant sees

1. The current hierarchy and actual counts, before allocation. Include current AI only when described, ordinary software accurately and unknown relationships visibly. Planned pilots belong in the proposal.
2. Each current position's work split, before staffing choices. Group by actual performers, never accountable owners. Keep AI/software and remaining human actions distinct; show justified interventions, empty human contributions and unexamined duties honestly. Shared activities are not independent workloads.
3. The final current/proposed hierarchy, after reassignment and staffing decisions. Show human posts and reporting levels separately, with explicit vertical and horizontal removals when chosen and supported. Agent roles and running instances are separate quantities. Agents appear beside their recorded user or supervisor through a labelled relationship.

Keep earlier readbacks available. Reuse supplied prior work rather than repeating discovery. A correction preserves unrelated answers, makes dependent conclusions provisional and receives a new revision.

The final comparison opens on the connected human-agent hierarchy. Its division, reporting and decision-authority views show the same recorded work and people before and after. The Workflow view shows the recorded sequence and decision boundaries. Full contributions, reasons, accountability and exceptions remain accessible in details. The detailed workbook records those facts, service maintenance, capability needs, readiness, the trial and unresolved questions.

Every comparison is changed, retained, newly specified or not assessed. Retained requires an examined choice; unknown past practice cannot establish a historical change. Known absence differs from unknown activity. Keep imported proposals attributed and provisional until accepted. Scope the result to the work examined; do not extrapolate one workflow to the whole organisation.

## Check what was produced

After generating a stage, inspect its content against the answers. Before final confirmation, open each view and the detailed workbook, not only the opening diagram. Verify:

- Counts describe human posts, not role types, agent roles or instances. Removed posts have a task destination or a recorded decision to discontinue work.
- Each recorded agent service appears with its human supervision relationship. Outcome ownership, maintenance and exception handling do not silently establish supervision. A multi-post supervisor role needs a particular post before drawing an individual link.
- Decision authority and current/proposed workflows are populated from actual answers. Normal permission, escalation, stop and override rights agree wherever repeated. Repair a missing record from known answers before requesting new information.
- The workbook's work-change and design-decision sections contain the same facts as the comparison. Projections do not replace checking prose fidelity.
- No human duty, approval, new control specialist or redundant post was invented. Repeated checking has not been removed merely because it repeats a check; independent error detection may need testing.
- A correction did not leave an obsolete claim in notes. Proposed access, service ownership and staffing approvals are not described as operational facts.

Read visible wording separately from design, including details and print text. Use literal, connected language, no italics or stock explanatory copy. Keep prompt logic, field editing and rendering commentary out of participant replies and artifacts. Preserve existing attribution and visual grammar.

When permitted browser tools are available, inspect actual HTML, keyboard controls, expanded details, phone width and print output. Check exported SVGs or images separately for text bounds, line direction, ownership, counts and legibility. SVG inspection does not prove HTML interaction or print behaviour. A fresh reader should describe the change from the visual without the intended answer. Report unperformed checks honestly; do not bypass a blocked tool.

The helper's `check.json` establishes the recorded stage's completeness and internal consistency, not company facts, capacity estimates or feasibility. `ready_for_confirmation` permits asking about record accuracy; it is not participant confirmation or company approval.

## Other hosts and incomplete sessions

Without Python, use native artifacts with the same sequence and bundled palette/actor conventions, or reporting trees and two-column tables in chat. No new account, connector, MCP, remote library or font is required. Keep questions and corrections in conversation and artifacts read-only. The participant never completes another questionnaire inside the workbook.

For a native or text-only final readback, perform the same checks: ordered workflows, populated decision comparison, visible service supervision, consistent work-change and design decisions, staffing choice and bounded trial. Unresolved historical practice stays unknown. If completion needs missing information, ask only the next material question or deliver a scoped draft if the participant stops. Do not fabricate answers to fill a view.

Deliver the final comparison prominently, the workbook when generated, and actual download links. Ask whether that revision accurately records the proposal only after checking it. Confirmation changes only the exercise record; it does not authorise personnel action, system access or deployment. A Print/save PDF control is not an already-created PDF; create and inspect a PDF only when requested. State once when downloadable files are unavailable.

## Existing records

Specialised renderers and version 1 contracts remain for existing records. New interviews use the session helper. Preserve legacy facts and add explicit service-to-group correspondences before adopting that path; labels cannot establish that two groups represent the same service. Do not force a participant into the old software-release example.
