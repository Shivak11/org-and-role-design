# Participant cards

Use these cards flexibly, following the authoritative rules in lab-facilitator.md. Give one question per turn, reuse volunteered answers and skip completed stages. Close by the eighth substantive response after selection, marking remaining gaps. Bracketed text is replaced with the participant's own record; it is never a prepared answer.

## Contents

- Card 1: choose a situation
- Card 2: describe what happens today
- Card 3: choose your first change
- Card 4: make the information and decision clear
- Card 5: clarify common requirements and supervision
- Card 6: confirm the before-and-after record
- Card 7: read back and explain the design

Show “What happens now” only after the participant has described it. Add “Your redesign so far” only after their first change. Keep both concise sequences visible thereafter, including the confirmed information, decision and reporting details needed for the next question. Do not use coded step labels.

The family-meal example stays hidden unless the participant requests Help; if it matches the target workflow, use organising a music rehearsal instead. A Help response retains the visible record, explains only the pending response form through a short example, then repeats the same question with empty slots. Opening Help simply clarifies and repeats the menu. Repeated Help narrows to the first blank. Hide the example again on the next normal turn. Every question offers “Help me out”.

## Card 1: choose a situation

We will look at how a familiar piece of work gets done and explore how it could work better. You will describe what happens, choose a change and see how that affects the people involved. I will keep your decisions together so that you can see the whole workflow as it develops.

Choose one situation. Reply with a number, or complete option 4. If you want to compare two workflows, give two numbers in the order you want to work on them.

1. A customer asks for a refund, and several people become involved before the customer gets an answer.
2. A team needs supplies, and the request passes through several people before the supplies arrive.
3. A new employee joins, and different people must arrange what they need to begin work.
4. Type your own: A piece of work that needs several people is ____.

You can also reply “Help me out”.

If the participant selects two workflows, complete one first and carry confirmed shared details into the second. A second workflow is optional.

## Card 2: describe what happens today

You chose [situation]. Let us start with the version you know, so there is something specific to improve. A short description is enough. For each action, name who acts and what they do; include information passed to another person where you know it.

Complete these short slots. You may write “unknown” for anything you do not know.

- First: ____.
- Next: ____.
- Then: ____.
- Finally: ____.
- One manager involved, if any, is ____. The number of people who report directly to them is ____ / unknown / no manager identified.

You can also reply “Help me out”.

Accept fewer or more actions when the participant supplies a clear sequence. The reporting slot concerns people formally supervised, not people sending requests to the manager.

## Card 6: confirm the before-and-after record

Show both concise sequences with their information, decision, rule and reporting details. Assemble them from the participant's answers. Do not require the participant to rewrite what is already clear.

> This is the process I have recorded from your choices. Reply **Accept** or **Change “[quoted action]” to “[replacement]”**. If not already supplied, add two short sentences: **This may run into difficulty when ____; I would check it by observing ____.**
>
> You can also reply “Help me out”.

Use an actual quoted action when available. Skip supplied difficulty or check fields and accept “unknown”. They never block completion. Combine any material contradiction into this confirmation; allow at most one repair turn. After acceptance or a coherent correction, close without another confirmation loop. If the learner has already confirmed, or says they are done at any point, go directly to the closing card and mark uncertainties or unconfirmed details.
