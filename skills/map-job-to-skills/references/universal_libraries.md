# Atom libraries

These are the fixed menus for the cognitive and behavioural pillars. Select 2 or 3 from each based on the job description. Do not invent new atoms; pick from these lists and write role-specific flavour text.

## Cognitive atoms (pick 2 or 3 per role)

| atom_id | atom_name | description | high_weight_roles |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| cog_structured_breakdown | Structured problem breakdown | Decompose complex, ambiguous problems into tractable sub-parts with clear sequencing | Consulting, presales, ops, law, project mgmt |
| cog_synthesis | Synthesis under ambiguity | Produce a single coherent output from multiple conflicting, incomplete, or contradictory inputs | Presales, brand mgmt, editorial, research, policy |
| cog_quantitative | Quantitative reasoning | Work with numbers: estimates, ratios, margins, unit economics, statistical interpretation | Finance, supply chain, analytics, data science |
| cog_pattern_recognition | Pattern recognition | Spot recurring structures, anomalies, or templates across data, code, cases, or situations | Engineering, data science, law, audit, QA |
| cog_creative_ideation | Creative ideation | Generate novel, non-obvious solutions or concepts under constraints | Marketing, design, advertising, product, R&D |
| cog_judgment | Judgment under uncertainty | Make defensible decisions with incomplete information and real consequences | Trading, medicine, crisis mgmt, leadership, law |
| cog_logical_argumentation | Logical argumentation | Build and evaluate structured arguments with evidence, premises, and conclusions | Law, policy, debate, academic research, compliance |

## Behavioural atoms (pick 2 or 3 per role)

| atom_id | atom_name | description | high_weight_roles |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| beh_communication | Communication clarity | Translate complex ideas across audiences with different technical literacy and priorities | Universal; flavor changes by audience type |
| beh_stakeholder_orchestration | Stakeholder orchestration | Coordinate, negotiate, and drive outcomes across multiple parties with competing incentives | Cross-functional roles, presales, PM, consulting |
| beh_organizational_fit | Organizational fit | Align personal working style with the company's culture, pace, ambiguity level, and expectations | Universal; flavor changes dramatically by company |
| beh_influence | Influence & negotiation | Persuade, negotiate, or align others toward a position without relying on formal authority | Sales, consulting, BD, client-facing, leadership |
| beh_collaboration | Collaboration orientation | Work productively within team structures, whether hierarchical, flat, cross-cultural, or remote | Universal; flavor set by org structure |
| beh_resilience | Resilience under pressure | Sustain quality output under tight deadlines, high stakes, or shifting goalposts | Startup, consulting, trading, journalism, medicine |
